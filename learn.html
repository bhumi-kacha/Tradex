Learn Trading

Understand the basics of stock trading before you trade.

📈

What is a Stock?

A stock represents a small ownership share in a company. When you buy a stock, you become a shareholder of that company.

🏦

What is Stock Market?

The stock market is a place where people buy and sell shares of companies.

🛒

What is BUY?

Buying means purchasing shares of a company with the expectation that their value may change in the future.

💰

What is SELL?

Selling means giving up shares that you own in exchange for their current value.

📊

Profit & Loss

Profit occurs when the selling value is higher than the purchase value. Loss occurs when it is lower.

💼

What is Portfolio?

A portfolio is a collection of investments owned by a person.

🎮

What is Paper Trading?

Paper trading allows you to practice buying and selling stocks using virtual money instead of real money.

⚠️

Risk Management

Risk management means understanding possible losses and avoiding decisions based only on expected profits.
